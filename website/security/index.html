How to Report

  1. Email security@p31ca.org with subject: [P31 Security] Brief description
  2. Encrypt with our PGP key for sensitive reports
  3. Include: affected URL/endpoint, steps to reproduce, impact assessment, your K₄ score estimate
  4. You'll receive an auto-acknowledgment with a BUG-YYYY-NNN tracking ID within 1 hour
  5. For active exploitation, reply URGENT — we escalate immediately

Safe Harbor

P31 Labs commits to the following for researchers acting in good faith:

  • We will not pursue legal action against you for responsible disclosure
  • We will not contact law enforcement for security research on in-scope systems
  • We will acknowledge your work in our Hall of Fame (with permission)
  • We will treat your report confidentially until coordinated disclosure

Good-faith research means: no modification or deletion of production data, no social engineering, no physical threats, no research targeting out-of-scope systems. This safe harbor applies to the extent permitted by applicable law.
