PROGRAM OVERVIEW
----------------
P31 Labs, Inc. (EIN 42-1888158) runs a responsible disclosure program
for security researchers. We classify vulnerabilities using a K4
topology severity matrix (physical/network/compliance/UX) and pay
bounties from $100 (LOW) to $2,500+ (CRITICAL).

IN SCOPE
--------
- https://phosphorus31.org/*
- https://p31ca.org/*
- https://bonding.p31ca.org/*
- https://donate-api.phosphorus31.org/*
- *.trimtab-signal.workers.dev (all Cloudflare Workers)
- https://github.com/p31labs/* (public repos)

OUT OF SCOPE — DO NOT TEST
---------------------------
- Stripe, Cloudflare, Google infrastructure
- Third-party dependencies (report to upstream vendor)
- Node Zero hardware (pre-production, not yet in scope)
- Rate-limiting / denial-of-service testing
- Social engineering of individuals

ABSOLUTELY OUT OF SCOPE (no exceptions)
-----------------------------------------
- Operator medical data (hypoparathyroidism, calcium monitoring)
- Children's mesh identities (S.J., W.J.)
- Physical safety of any individuals

SAFE HARBOR
-----------
P31 Labs will not pursue legal action or contact law enforcement
against researchers acting in good faith on in-scope systems.
Good faith requires: no data modification, no social engineering,
no physical threats, testing only in-scope targets.
